Workspace sources/prts/downloads.json records exact source URLs, byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes.
The offline preparation scripts reconstruct only setup-pose geometry and render ordinary PNG images.
The game uses twelve Exusiai image parts with measured source bone anchors and one Slug image for patrol drones.
N's movement poses are retargeted to the image proportions; original full animation tracks are not played.
No Spine animation runtime or Arknights animation/AI system is included in the executable.
The format-reference Java files under sources/prts/reference retain their original licenses and are not exported.
